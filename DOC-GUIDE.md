# Doctor / Clinical Staff Guide

## Purpose

ShasthoPath provides a short pre-consultation intake summary.

The doctor may see:

- Patient identity
- Chief complaint
- Duration
- Patient-reported history
- Available reports
- Basic measured vitals
- Follow-up status
- Queue/token

## Important

The information is patient-reported or entered during intake.

It is NOT a diagnosis.

The doctor remains responsible for:

- Clinical assessment
- Diagnosis
- Investigation
- Treatment
- Prescription
- Follow-up decision

## Queue

The doctor can:

- Call next
- Recall
- Skip
- Complete
- Transfer

## Follow-Up

Existing scheduled patients can be routed through the follow-up workflow instead of unnecessarily repeating the entire registration process.

## Unknown Values

Unknown information must remain unknown.

Do not assume or infer missing values from incomplete intake data.
