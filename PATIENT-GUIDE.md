# Patient Guide

## What ShasthoPath Does

ShasthoPath helps patients understand:

- What to do
- Where to go
- Which service they may need
- Where their queue is
- When their token is called
- What happens next

## How To Use

### 1. Start

Touch:

"আমি চিকিৎসা নিতে চাই"

### 2. Describe Your Problem

You can say:

"আমার মাথা ব্যথা।"

or

"আমার হাতে ব্যথা।"

### 3. Answer Questions

Answer only what you know.

If you do not know something, say:

"জানি না।"

The system records it as unknown.

### 4. Receive Token

Keep the printed ticket.

### 5. Follow Directions

The ticket tells you:

- Building
- Floor
- Room
- Counter
- Department

### 6. Wait

Watch the queue display.

When your token is called, proceed to the indicated location.

## Important

ShasthoPath does not replace a doctor.

If you feel seriously unwell or have an emergency, seek emergency medical help immediately.
