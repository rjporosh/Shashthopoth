# Patient Guide

## What ShasthoPath Does

ShasthoPath helps patients understand:

- What to do
- Where to go
- Which service they may need
- Where their queue is
- When their token is called
- What happens next

This is a **demonstration**. Please do not enter real sensitive medical information.

## How To Use

### 1. Start

Touch:

"আমি চিকিৎসা নিতে চাই" (I need treatment)

Other big buttons: "আমার পরবর্তী তারিখ বা ইনজেকশন আছে" (I have a scheduled visit or injection), "কোথায় কী আছে" (Where is what? — toilet, food, pharmacy, billing, reports), and the red "জরুরি" (Emergency) button.

You can change the language with the "English / বাংলা" button at the top.

### 2. Describe Your Problem

Touch the 🎤 button and say:

"আমার মাথা ব্যথা।" or "আমার হাতে ব্যথা।"

If the microphone does not work, **touch a picture** instead — it always works. You can also type.

Came for something else (report, medicine, bill, test, X-ray)? Touch the picture under "অন্য কাজে এসেছি".

### 3. Answer Questions

Each question has big answer buttons. Answer only what you know.

If you do not know something, touch or say:

"জানি না।"

The system records it as unknown. It never guesses.

If you have something serious (heavy bleeding, trouble breathing, fainting, severe chest pain, a serious accident, a sudden severe allergy, a fit), touch it on the first question screen — you will be sent to Emergency.

### 4. See Where To Go

The screen tells you which service is usually the right place to start. This is **not a diagnosis**. If you want another service, touch "অন্য সেবা বেছে নিই".

### 5. Tell Us About You

Name, age, sex and mobile number — only what you know. Touch "জানি না" for age if unsure. In the next screen you may add weight, blood pressure, temperature, sugar, and any illness, medicine or allergy **you already know about**. Leave blank if unknown; the doctor will see "Unknown".

### 6. Receive Token

Keep the printed ticket (touch "টিকিট ছাপুন"). It shows your token, service, doctor (if any), building, floor, room, how many people are ahead, and the time.

### 7. Follow Directions

Touch "পথ দেখুন" for a map and simple steps: building, floor and room. Touch 🔊 to hear it.

### 8. Wait

Watch the queue display. The screen on your token shows how many people are ahead (estimate only). When it turns green and says "আপনার ডাক এসেছে", go to the room shown.

### 9. After The Doctor

Do what the doctor tells you. Tests → Laboratory, medicine → Pharmacy, reports and bills have their own desks (buttons on the token screen show where).

### 10. Follow-up Visit

If the doctor gave you a next date: on that day touch "আমার পরবর্তী তারিখ বা ইনজেকশন আছে", enter your mobile number, check that the name shown is yours, and touch "হ্যাঁ, আমি". You get a follow-up token — **no new registration**. If you come before your date, the screen tells you the date to come.

## Important

ShasthoPath does not replace a doctor.

If you feel seriously unwell or have an emergency, seek emergency medical help immediately — tell any staff member or go straight to the Emergency department.
